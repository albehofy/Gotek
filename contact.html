Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does a project typically take?+
Timelines vary by service. Branding projects typically take 2–3 weeks. Websites take 3–6 weeks. Ad campaigns launch within 1 week. POS systems are delivered in 4–8 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline in every proposal.
Do you work with businesses outside Saudi Arabia?+
Absolutely. We serve clients across the Middle East and North Africa, including Egypt, UAE, Kuwait, and beyond. Most of our work is delivered remotely, so location is never a barrier.
What's included in the free consultation?+
Our free consultation includes a 30–45 minute call where we analyze your business, discuss your goals, evaluate your current digital presence, and recommend the best strategy. No cost, no commitment.
Do you offer monthly retainer packages?+
Yes. We offer flexible monthly retainers for ongoing services like social media management, paid advertising, and maintenance. These can include multiple services at a bundled rate for long-term partners.
How do I pay and what's the payment structure?+
We typically work on a 50% deposit to begin, 50% on delivery model for project-based work. Monthly services are billed at the start of each month. We accept bank transfer and all major digital payment methods.
Can you handle multiple services simultaneously?+
Yes — and this is where Gotek truly shines. Having a single partner managing your brand identity, website, ads, social media, and POS system ensures consistency and efficiency. Many clients start with one service and expand as they see results.
